Four ways to answer an email. Only one of them costs money.

invoice

clarify

Something's missing. Ask for exactly that, nothing else.

decline

On our won't-make list: weapons, logos, hate symbols. Said politely.

answer

It's a question, not an order. Answered from the policy only.

The guardrails

A written policy, and code that won't let it slip.

Prices come from policy.md

Or a price our jeweller confirmed, and never below materials cost. Never what the customer says it should be.

The email is untrusted

Wrapped in <untrusted_email>. Instructions inside it are data, not commands.

The FAQ inbox can't bill

Enforced in code, not just the prompt: whatever the model says, faq@ never reaches Stripe.

No double billing

Every Stripe call's idempotency key comes from the email id, so webhook retries are harmless.
