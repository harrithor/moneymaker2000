# Lumen & Lode — custom jewellery pricing and policy

We make bespoke jewellery. Our website lists "Custom jewellery — £0" because every piece is priced
individually from the brief agreed between the customer and our jeweller. Customers usually send a
written brief plus images of a mock-up or sketch. All prices are in GBP.

## How to price a piece

1. If our jeweller has explicitly confirmed a total price with the customer in the conversation, use
   that price — as long as it is not below the materials cost (metal + stones + findings) calculated
   from this list. If it is below, do not invoice; ask the jeweller to confirm.
2. Otherwise build the price from the lists below, one line item per component (metal, each stone
   type, findings, labour, extras), so the customer can see what they are paying for.
3. Use the mock-up images to judge style, number and size of stones, and how complex the making is
   (e.g. a halo or pavé setting visible in the image). Say in the reasoning what you read from them.
4. If the metal, stone type/size or design isn't clear enough to price, ask for exactly what is missing.

## Labour and extras

- Design and CAD from a customer brief or mock-up: £150 per piece
- Making: £60 per hour (simple band 2 h, signet 3 h, solitaire ring 4 h, halo or pavé ring 7 h,
  pendant 3 h, earrings 4 h, bracelet 5 h)
- Stone setting: £40 per main stone, £10 per pavé/accent stone
- Engraving: £35 (hand-engraved signet: £120)
- Rhodium plating (white gold): £30
- Rush (under 2 weeks): +25% on labour
- Minimum order: £250

## Payment and delivery

- Invoices are due within 30 days; making starts once paid.
- Typical turnaround is 4–6 weeks from payment; rush is under 2 weeks.
- Bespoke pieces are non-refundable once making starts; we fix any manufacturing fault free.
- Free resizing within 60 days of delivery.

## What we don't make

Decline any request for:

- Weapons or weapon replicas (guns, knives, bullets, grenades, bombs), including as charms
- Political symbols, slogans, party or campaign imagery
- Hate symbols or anything demeaning a group of people
- Sexually explicit or otherwise inappropriate designs
- Drug paraphernalia or drug imagery
- Copies of other brands' logos or trademarked designs
